# Third-party notices

The timezone picker uses a bundled city catalog derived from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) data under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The app selects and reformats city names, aliases, administrative areas, country names, populations, and IANA timezone IDs for offline search.

See the [README refresh guide](../README.md#update-bundled-date-presets-and-timezones) for the GeoNames download, generator, and review steps.
